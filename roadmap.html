The Climb

The Roadmap

Four levels. Clear metrics. Hit the number, earn the promotion — no politics, no guessing.

Level 01

Setter

Book the appointments.

You're at the doors, setting qualified appointments and learning the game from the ground up.

Promotion: 15 accounts in 12 weeks → become a Self-Gen

Level 02

Self-Gen

Generate and build.

You're creating your own opportunities and starting to build a team beneath you.

Promotion: 20 self-gen accounts + 2+ recruits → become a Closer

Level 03

Closer

Close and recruit.

You own the full sale and start building a setter team of your own.

Promotion: Recruit 5 setters → become a Manager

Level 04

Manager

Lead and scale.

You've built a team and stepped into leadership — now you earn on everything they produce.

Earning: $150/kW override per install
